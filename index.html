About Me

I’m Irene Castro, a Filipino-American data science student pursuing a Master’s in Data Science & Analytics with a concentration in Engineering and Big Data. I was born in New Jersey and have lived in Cary, North Carolina since 2004. I hold Bachelor of Science degrees in Information Systems and Business Administration from UNC Greensboro (2019).

I’m driven by a deeper understanding of how data shapes the world, from the products we build to the decisions we make every day. As a Porting Specialist in Technical Operations, I’m applying these skills directly to my work. I chose to pursue my Master’s in Data Science because I hope to inspire other women to see themselves in STEM.

Outside of work and school, I enjoy exploring coffee shops, giving back to my community, and spending time with family and friends. A fun fact: I performed at Lucas Oil Stadium three times with my high school marching band at a national competition.
